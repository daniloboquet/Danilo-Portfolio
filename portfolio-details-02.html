Project Overview

  • Category: UX/UI Design
  • Client: Zeus Arsenal
  • Role: Lead UX Designer
  • Duration: 6 months
  • Skills: UX/UI Design, eCommerce Strategy, User Research, Wireframing, Prototyping, Content and Media Management, Magento 2 Implementation, Usability Testing

Redefining the Zeus Arsenal Digital Experience

Zeus Arsenal produces high-performance vaporizers, but their online presence didn’t match their cutting-edge technology. The challenge? Transforming an outdated WordPress site into a premium eCommerce experience that aligns with the quality of their products.

My goal was to reimagine the UX/UI, enhance eCommerce capabilities, and refine brand positioning—all while migrating the platform to Magento 2 for better scalability and performance.

The Problem: A Website That Didn’t Match the Brand

1. Outdated Look & Feel

  • The old site failed to reflect the premium nature of Zeus Arsenal’s products.
  • Lack of visual hierarchy and engaging storytelling reduced customer trust.

2. Clunky Shopping Experience

  • Users struggled to find and compare products easily.
  • Checkout friction led to drop-offs before completing a purchase. The old website redirected to a different uRL to complete checkout.

3. Limited Scalability & Performance Issues

  • The WordPress platform was not optimized for a high-traffic eCommerce store.
  • Performance issues affected page load speed and mobile usability.

The Solution: A Strategic UX Overhaul

I approached this project with a user-first design methodology, combining research, UX/UI strategy, and eCommerce optimization to elevate Zeus Arsenal into a true premium brand.

1. Understanding Our Users

I conducted extensive user research to identify pain points, needs, and expectations:

  • User Interviews & Surveys → Gathered insights from existing customers.
  • Competitor Analysis → Benchmarked best practices from industry leaders.
  • User Personas & Journeys → Designed data-driven personas to guide decision-making.

2. Wireframing, Prototyping & User Testing

Before jumping into UI design, I created low-fidelity wireframes to outline the new user flow:

  • Designed an intuitive product discovery experience with improved navigation.
  • Optimized the checkout process for speed and ease of use.
  • Conducted usability testing to validate design decisions.

3. UI & Visual Design Refresh

To align the website with Zeus Arsenal’s premium status, I crafted a sleek, modern design system:

  • Dark, high-contrast theme → Reflecting innovation & high performance.
  • Cinematic product imagery & microinteractions → Enhancing engagement and visual appeal.
  • Refined typography & iconography → Creating a polished, trustworthy experience.

4. Magento 2 Migration & Performance Enhancements

Since the existing WordPress setup couldn’t meet the client's necessities, I spearheaded the migration to Magento 2:

  • Implemented a robust eCommerce platform with better performance & security.
  • Created and optimized an internal checkout flow to reduce friction and cart abandonment.
  • Ensured a mobile-first approach for an effortless experience on any device.

The Results: A Premium Experience That Converts

  • 65% increase in conversion rates.
  • Higher engagement → Users spent more time exploring products.
  • Faster and reliable checkout experience → Reduced friction, improved sales.
  • Future-proofed scalability → the Magento 2 platform ensures long-term growth.

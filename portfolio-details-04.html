Transforming an Outdoor & Motorsports eCommerce Giant

Royal Distributing, a leading retailer in motorsports and outdoor gear, needed continuous improvements to enhance its user experience, conversion rates, and site performance. During my 3.5-year tenure, I led multiple UX/UI and development projects, refining the website's functionality and optimizing the eCommerce experience.

The Challenges: Enhancing UX & Performance in a Fast-Paced Retail Environment

  • Limited user engagement → The site lacked key eCommerce features like product reviews and A/B testing.
  • Checkout friction → No clear shipping estimates, leading to cart abandonment.
  • Search & filtering inefficiencies → Users struggled to find products quickly.
  • SEO & performance issues → Heavy images and poor optimization impacted rankings & speed.

The Strategy: Continuous UX Optimization for a Seamless Shopping Experience

1. Product Reviews & Social Proof Integration

To build customer trust and increase conversions, I:

  • Integrated a product review system → Boosted credibility and user engagement.
  • Designed a clean, intuitive UI for review submission & browsing.
  • Monitored analytics → Increased conversion rates on reviewed products.

2. Checkout UX Enhancements (Shipping Quotes & Trust Signals)

To decrease cart abandonment, I:

  • Added real-time shipping quotes at checkout for cost transparency.
  • Improved form design & error handling for a smoother process.
  • Conducted A/B testing on checkout flows → Optimized for higher completion rates.

3. Smart Search & Advanced Filtering System

To enhance product discovery, I:

  • Implemented a smart predictive search for faster, more relevant results.
  • Redesigned category filters to improve navigation & findability.
  • Conducted usability testing → Ensured the new system met user needs.

4. A/B Testing & Landing Page Optimization

To refine user experience and marketing effectiveness, I:

  • Designed & coded high-converting sales pages.
  • Integrated A/B testing tools to experiment with layouts & CTAs.
  • Measured engagement → Optimized based on heatmaps & analytics.

5. SEO Optimization & Performance Improvements

To boost organic traffic and site speed, I:

  • Optimized product images & site assets → Faster loading times.
  • Implemented SEO best practices → Structured metadata, keyword optimization.
  • Monitored & tested site performance regularly → Improved Core Web Vitals.

The Results: A More Engaging & High-Performing eCommerce Platform

  • Higher engagement & conversions → Product reviews increased customer trust.
  • Faster, more intuitive checkout → Shipping transparency reduced cart abandonment.
  • Improved search & filtering → Users found products more easily.
  • Stronger SEO & performance → Faster loading speeds & better rankings.

Final Thoughts: A Continuous UX Evolution

At Royal Distributing, I played a key role in optimizing and evolving the eCommerce experience over 3.5 years. From feature implementations to performance improvements, UX research, and A/B testing, my work directly contributed to a smoother, faster, and more user-friendly website.

Next Steps: Further UX optimizations based on evolving user behavior and retail trends.
